1. Defect found and fixed in this session

D1 — Customer refresh returned 404 where the staff plane returns 401

Symptom. Every anonymous page load on the customer site logged
404 POST /api/v1/customer/auth/refresh. Because refreshSession() classifies
401/403 as "no session" and everything else as "transient outage", the 404
was read as an outage: restoreCustomerSession() retried three times with
backoff. A first-time visitor's homepage therefore issued three failed
requests before rendering.

Root cause. CustomerAuthController.refresh threw
notFound(ERROR_CODES.AUTH_TOKEN_INVALID, 'No refresh token was presented.')
when no refresh cookie was present, while the equivalent staff handler in
AuthController.refresh throws unauthorized(...). The route exists in both
cases; the caller simply has no session. 404 was also semantically wrong — a
missing credential is not a missing resource.

Fix. apps/api/src/customer/customer-auth.controller.ts now throws
unauthorized(...) for the same condition, matching the staff plane.

8. Phase E — light-theme correction

Phase E corrected the light theme the owner reported as inconsistent, and in
doing so found eight defects. The customer site had defaulted to the dark
theme rather than the approved light reference (E0); gold was one token doing
two incompatible jobs (E1); the ink on a gold fill was not the approved dark
ink (E2); the hero, header and panel scrims were built from the page field
rather than the navy band the approved reference uses (E3); the profile
"Delete" control rendered the raw key common.delete (E4); the authenticated
captures had silently shot the login page (E5, an evidence defect); a
category-card link failed WCAG contrast at 4.28:1 on its tinted scrim (E7,
found by an exhaustive rendered-contrast sweep rather than a fixed list, and the
defect the owner's complaint was actually pointing at); and two pre-existing dark
pairs sit below 4.5:1 (E8, reported and deliberately left alone so the
approved dark theme stays intact).

The light theme is now an ivory page with white and warm-neutral surfaces, deep
charcoal type, a metallic gold accent and a navy hero/footer band — all driven by
central tokens in packages/shared/src/tokens.ts and mirrored into
:root[data-theme='light'], with the dark block defining the same token names so
no rule needs a theme branch. The dark theme's palette is unchanged: the
theme token layer that paints the header chrome is identical across all 31 dark
captures, and the whole-frame differences are confined to regions that carry CMS
photography (see §16 of the Phase E report). Full detail, evidence and the test
results are in 04_PHASE_E_LIGHT_THEME.md.
